## 完整模板

```
Subject and Composition
A minimalist and elegant Jummah Mubarak greeting card design centered on a serene architectural composition. The scene features the iconic white dome and two minarets of Al-Masjid an-Nabawi rendered in a clean, sophisticated aesthetic. A subtle, modern elevator glass lift is integrated into the side of the mosque structure, featuring sleek brushed steel and transparent glass panels that reflect the surrounding courtyard light. Tiny, graceful silhouettes of birds are scattered near the dome, adding a sense of movement and scale.

Typography and Calligraphy
At the top, prominent bold black Arabic calligraphy reads Jummah Mubarak, with the English translation placed directly underneath in a crisp, modern sans-serif font. At the bottom, exquisite Arabic calligraphy displays the Quranic verse فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا, followed by its English transliteration and the text So, surely with hardship comes ease, maintaining a clear and balanced visual hierarchy.

Lighting and Atmosphere
Soft, diffused daylight creates a calm and spiritual atmosphere, emphasizing the clean lines of the architecture. The lighting is high-key and even, avoiding harsh shadows to maintain a professional, corporate, and modern feel. The air feels crisp and still, with a sense of quiet reverence.

Material and Color Palette
The background is a textured light grey, reminiscent of high-end stationery or premium paper stock. The color palette is strictly monochromatic grayscale, utilizing subtle gradients and clean whites, deep blacks, and soft greys to ensure a cohesive and sophisticated look.

Constraints
No oversaturated colors, no vibrant neons, no plastic textures, no distorted architectural elements, no blurry or low-resolution text, no extra watermarks or logos. Ensure the calligraphy is sharp and legible, and the integration of the lift is seamless and architecturally consistent.
```
